# User guide

## First launch

1. Read or skip the three local-first onboarding pages.
2. Create a local profile by choosing a name, avatar, and accent.
3. Add an optional four-digit PIN or choose **Skip for now**.
4. The Home screen opens with quick access to Stockfish, pass-and-play, puzzles, progress, and the most recent game.

## Play Stockfish

Open **Play**, select **Stockfish**, then choose:

- White, Black, or random side;
- Easy, Medium, or Hard strength;
- no clock, 3 minutes, 5 minutes, or 10 minutes;
- hint availability and legal-move markers.

Tap or drag a piece. A selected square is highlighted, legal destinations use dots, and captures use rings. The last move remains highlighted. Stockfish calculation happens on a background worker so the board remains responsive.

## Pass and play

Choose **Pass & Play** from Home or Play. Both sides share the device. The board orientation can be flipped at any time.

## During a game

The bottom controls provide:

- **Undo** — rewinds a full turn against Stockfish or one ply in pass-and-play;
- **Hint** — asks the local engine for a move and draws a hint arrow;
- **Flip** — reverses board orientation;
- **Analyze** — opens the current position in local analysis;
- **More** — restart, resign, export, or return Home.

Promotion opens a chooser for queen, rook, bishop, or knight unless Auto-Queen is enabled.

## Check and checkmate

Check marks the king square in amber. Checkmate changes the king square and board frame to red, shows the result sheet, and offers Rematch, Analyze, Export, and Home. Haptics remain brief and can be disabled in Settings.

## Puzzles

The five packaged tactics require no download. A correct solution updates the active profile's solved count and streak. An incorrect move leaves the position intact and invites another attempt.

## History, replay, and analysis

Completed games appear under **History**. Open one to replay every ply, flip the board, or analyze the selected position with Stockfish. Analysis reports evaluation, depth, best move, and principal variation locally.

## Profiles and local lock

Up to four local profiles can share one device. Profile data includes ratings, game results, and puzzle progress. The optional PIN is a convenience boundary, not cloud authentication or strong protection from the device owner.

## Local export

Export writes game metadata and the UCI move log to Android's app-specific external-files directory. No broad storage permission is required.

## Resetting

**Settings → Reset Local Data** erases profiles, settings, active games, history, and puzzle progress after confirmation.
