# Framilton Chess identity

Framilton Chess uses a restrained white-and-blue identity aligned with the broader Framilton product family.

## Name

- Primary product name: **Framilton Chess**.
- Masthead treatment: **FRAMILTON**.
- Android package: `com.framilton.knight`.
- Release artifact: `FramiltonChess-v3-offline-arm64.apk`.

## Visual direction

- Deep navy surfaces for focus and contrast.
- Clear blue as the primary identity accent.
- White typography and marks on dark or blue surfaces.
- Chess imagery should remain geometric and functional rather than ornamental.
- The interface should prioritize board state, move legibility, and local/offline trust cues over decorative branding.

## Product principles

- Local-first and private by default.
- No ads, analytics, telemetry, or account requirement.
- Framilton identity appears in launcher, splash, Android labels, release assets, and repository presentation.
- Stockfish is credited as the chess engine and remains visually distinct from the product identity.
